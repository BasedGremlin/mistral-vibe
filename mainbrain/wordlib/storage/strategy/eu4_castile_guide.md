# EU4 Castile / Spain: Master Strategy Guide

## Opening Priorities (1444 Start)

### Day 1 Checklist
1. **Ally Portugal** -- mission tree requires them, and they protect your flank
2. **Rival France and England** -- you need rivals to claim CB on them later
3. **Set Aragon as enemy** -- marriage then inherit, don't war yet
4. **Start Influence idea group** -- needed for PU (Personal Union) mechanics

### First War: Granada (1444-1446)
- Granada is isolated and weak. CB: Reconquista (free from missions)
- Siege all forts, take the full land
- Convert immediately -- every dev in Iberia needs Catholicism for bonuses
- Do NOT release Castile cores on taken land

### The Aragon Inheritance
- Marry Aragon (usually happens automatically via mission)
- Required: Be at peace, have positive relations, senior dynasty
- Triggers ~1470s if conditions met
- Gives you Aragon, Naples, and cores on Italy → huge dev jump

## Mission Tree Priorities

### Tier 1 Missions (complete ASAP)
1. **Iberian Wedding** -- inherit Aragon, unlocks Spain tag
2. **Reconquista** -- take Granada, free CB
3. **Secure Navarre** -- ally or vassal, gives cores

### Tier 2 (post-Aragon)
4. **Portuguese Alliance** -- lock in ally
5. **Italian Claims** -- CB on Italian minors
6. **Unite Iberia** -- final Iberian consolidation

### Colonial Missions (highest priority after 1490)
- Get explorer idea (Exploration ideas tier 1)
- Send conquistador to Americas immediately
- First colony: Cuba or Hispaniola (Caribbean for trade node)
- Second: Florida or Brazil coast
- Goal: Control the "Seville" and "Caribbean" trade nodes

---

## Colonization Strategy

### When to Colonize
- Start exploring after taking Granada (~1450-1460)
- Need Exploration ideas for colonists
- First colonist → nearest unclaimed coastal province

### Optimal Colonial Route
1. Canary Islands (if not owned) -- stepping stone
2. Caribbean coast -- tap the rich Caribbean trade node
3. Central America -- silver mines (Potosi area by 1545)
4. Brazil coast -- prevent Portugal from monopolizing

### Trade Route Priority
Caribbean → Seville → protect Seville node at all costs

### Colonial Nations
- Set up Colonial Nations in Americas to auto-colonize
- They feed you trade power and troops
- Keep their autonomy low (<50%) using Influence ideas

---

## Military Strategy

### Army Composition (early)
- 14 infantry, 4 cavalry, 4 artillery (when available)
- Quality > quantity in early game
- Spanish tercios (unique unit) unlocked via missions

### Fort Strategy
- Build forts only on borders, not interior
- Mountain forts block enemy movement efficiently
- Sell interior forts when not at war (maintenance cost)

### Navy Priority
- Need navy for colonization and protecting trade
- Heavy ships protect trade; light ships pirate enemy trade
- 20 galleys for Mediterranean warfare

---

## Economic Priorities

### Tax vs. Production vs. Trade
1. **Trade income** is king -- always prioritize Trade ideas
2. **Production** on gold/silver provinces compounds fast
3. **Tax** is weakest return on investment

### Development Priorities
- Develop only when you have 50%+ institution spread
- Focus on provinces with high base production
- Never over-develop a single province (diminishing returns after 20)

### Idea Groups Priority Order
1. Exploration (colonization access)
2. Influence (PU and vassal management)
3. Economic OR Trade (depending on playstyle)
4. Diplomatic (if going wide)
5. Administrative (tech cost reduction)

---

## Diplomacy and Expansion

### The Reputation Loop
- Aggressive Expansion (AE) builds over time
- AE causes coalitions -- the #1 game-ender
- Rule: Wait for AE to decay before next war
- Max AE before next war: ~50 across all affected nations

### When to Form Spain
- Requires: Own Castile, Aragon, Granada cores + Iberian Union of Portugal OR ownership
- Spain tag gives massive prestige bonus and unique events
- Usually achievable by ~1475-1490

### Coalition Avoidance
- Never take more than 50% of an enemy's provinces in one peace deal
- Use vassals to store excess land (release and feed)
- Always check the coalition map mode before signing peace

---

## Common Mistakes (avoid these)

1. **Warring Aragon before the PU fires** -- kills the inheritance
2. **Ignoring merchant republic trade rivals** -- Venice and Genoa eat your trade income
3. **Colonizing in Africa/Asia before Americas** -- Americas has better value early
4. **Letting Portugal ally France** -- blocks the Iberian Union path
5. **No explorers** -- can't colonize without them; research Exploration ideas first
