# Supplement Protocol: Evidence-Based Reference

## Tier 1: High Evidence, Daily Use

### Magnesium Glycinate
- **Dose**: 300-400mg elemental magnesium, evening
- **Why glycinate**: Best absorbed form, doesn't cause GI issues like oxide
- **Effects**: Sleep quality, muscle relaxation, stress response, migraine prevention
- **Timing**: 30-60 min before bed
- **Note**: Most people are deficient; soil depletion has reduced food sources

### Vitamin D3 + K2
- **Dose**: D3 2000-5000 IU + K2 100mcg (MK-7 form), with fat
- **Why together**: D3 increases calcium absorption; K2 directs it to bones not arteries
- **Testing**: Optimal serum level 50-80 ng/mL; test every 6 months
- **Tropical caveat**: In Philippines with regular sun exposure, may need less

### Omega-3 (EPA/DHA)
- **Dose**: 2-3g combined EPA+DHA daily, with food
- **Source**: Fish oil or algae-based (vegan)
- **Effects**: Anti-inflammatory, cardiovascular, cognitive, mood
- **Quality**: Look for IFOS certification; refrigerate after opening
- **Ratio**: Higher EPA for mood/inflammation; higher DHA for brain

### Creatine Monohydrate
- **Dose**: 3-5g daily, any time, no loading phase needed
- **Effects**: Strength, power output, cognitive function (well-studied)
- **Form**: Monohydrate is the gold standard; ignore fancy forms
- **Safety**: One of the most researched supplements; safe long-term

## Tier 2: Good Evidence, Context-Dependent

### Zinc
- **Dose**: 15-25mg elemental zinc, with food (NOT on empty stomach)
- **Forms**: Zinc picolinate or bisglycinate absorb best
- **Balance**: Take with copper (1-2mg) if supplementing long-term
- **Effects**: Immune function, testosterone, skin, wound healing
- **Testing**: Can test via serum zinc; many deficient

### B-Complex (or B12 separately)
- **B12**: Methylcobalamin 500-1000mcg (sublingual for best absorption)
- **Why methylcobalamin**: More bioavailable than cyanocobalamin
- **Critical if**: Vegan/vegetarian, over 50, or on metformin
- **Effects**: Energy metabolism, nerve function, mood

### Ashwagandha (KSM-66 extract)
- **Dose**: 300-600mg KSM-66 extract, with food
- **Effects**: Cortisol reduction, stress resilience, sleep quality, testosterone
- **Cycling**: Use 8-12 weeks on, 4 weeks off
- **Caution**: Avoid if thyroid issues (stimulates thyroid)

### L-Theanine
- **Dose**: 100-200mg with caffeine (2:1 ratio theanine:caffeine)
- **Effects**: Removes caffeine jitters while keeping focus; solo use = calm alertness
- **Timing**: With morning coffee; or 200mg alone in evening for relaxation
- **Safety**: Very safe, no dependency

## Tier 3: Targeted Use

### NAC (N-Acetyl Cysteine)
- **Dose**: 600-1200mg, away from meals
- **Use**: Glutathione precursor, liver support, respiratory, OCD reduction
- **Caution**: Discontinue before surgery; avoid with nitroglycerin

### Berberine
- **Dose**: 500mg 2-3x daily with meals
- **Effects**: Blood sugar regulation (comparable to metformin in some studies)
- **Note**: Take 2 weeks off every 2 months (may reduce gut microbiome diversity)
- **Interaction**: Cytochrome P450 inhibitor -- check drug interactions

### Rhodiola Rosea
- **Dose**: 200-400mg standardized extract (3% rosavins, 1% salidroside)
- **Effects**: Adaptogen, mental fatigue, physical endurance
- **Timing**: Morning or pre-exercise; may be stimulating
- **Cycling**: 6 weeks on, 1-2 weeks off

## Quality Markers

### What to look for on labels
- Third-party tested (NSF, Informed Sport, USP, IFOS)
- Standardized extracts (not raw herb powder)
- No proprietary blends (can't verify doses)
- Certificate of Analysis (CoA) available on request

### Red flags
- "Proprietary blend" without doses
- Extreme claims ("lose 30 lbs in 30 days")
- No third-party testing
- Extremely cheap fish oil (oxidation risk)

---

## Important Notes

- Supplements complement, not replace: whole food diet, sleep, exercise, sunlight
- Introduce one at a time: wait 2-3 weeks to assess effects before adding another
- Blood work baseline before heavy supplementation
- Consult a doctor for: pre-existing conditions, pregnancy, prescription medications
- Storage: most supplements prefer cool, dark, dry conditions
